## Recording tips
- Record on a phone in a quiet room, holding the phone about 20 cm away. Read one scene at a time; it's easier to place each piece separately in CapCut.
- If a scene's narration runs long, she can stretch that scene in CapCut, or cut words from it (the 0:49 and 1:04 scenes have the most text).
- Music: pick a calm instrumental (oud or ney) from Instagram's library at 10–15% volume under her voice.
- Cover image: use the opening frame ("Derslerimizi nasıl işliyoruz?"), `Arapca-Atolye-Reel-kapak.jpg`.
